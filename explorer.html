Scientific method

Maps use small, fixed-stretch WebP quicklooks derived reproducibly from each source TIFF for fast display. Exact reflectance, SCL and index values remain read from the untouched GeoTIFF. Quality percentages cover the complete raster footprint. Scene medians exclude obscured, no-data and saturated/defective pixels. No stress, disease, yield or phenological stage is inferred.
