Scientific guardrail

This simulator generates model-derived canopy surface reflectance using RTM-lite, PROSAIL-parameterized approximation. It is intended for interactive understanding of parameter effects. It is not measured HYDRA-EO data, not observed satellite data, not a validated PROSAIL run, and not a substitute for sensor-calibrated satellite data.
