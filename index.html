04 · Scientific problem

Crop stress signals overlap, shift with scale and resist simple attribution

Biotic and abiotic stress can produce similar spectral, thermal and fluorescence responses. Those responses also change with crop, environment, acquisition geometry, sensor characteristics and spatial support. HYDRA-EO is designed to test where physics-informed modelling and empirical observations can improve separation and transfer across platforms.

  1. RQ1Which spectral, thermal and fluorescence traits can separate drought, disease and nutrient or pest effects?
  2. RQ2How do candidate stress signals change from leaf and plot scale to UAV, airborne and satellite scale?
  3. RQ3Which sensor configurations are sufficient for early detection and scientifically defensible attribution?
  4. RQ4How can RTM-informed hybrid machine learning improve interpretability and transferability?
  5. RQ5Which validated outputs could support future ESA agricultural monitoring services?

08 · Data, provenance and readiness

Detected is not ready. Ready is not publication-ready.

HYDRA-EO handles raster, vector, tabular, spectral, metadata and provenance records. Raw sources are imported locally; lightweight browser assets may be published; large products require external HTTPS COG/STAC hosting.

Metadata contract

Band identity, wavelengths or roles, units, scale, offset, nodata, calibration, processing level, sensor, platform, date, CRS, formula, provenance and citation/contact.

Scientific guardrail

NDVI, NDRE, GNDVI, SAVI, PRI, thermal proxies, disease proxies, anomalies and change metrics are blocked in the browser until the full contract is established.

Import pathway

data-to-import/ → safe inspection → ignored data-imported/ archive → generated catalog → approved browser path or external reference.
