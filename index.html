Mission & Objectives

Our mission is to develop, validate, and share open methods for monitoring vegetation function, biodiversity, and climate impacts using multi-source satellite data and radiative transfer modelling.

Objective 1

Multi-scale monitoring

Combine satellite, airborne, and in-situ observations to derive functional traits across local, regional, and continental scales.

Objective 2

Advanced modelling

Use radiative transfer models and machine learning to link spectral signals with key ecosystem processes.

Objective 3

Open science

Release code, data, and reproducible workflows for the research and user community.

Featured Projects

A selection of ongoing activities demonstrating the use of space data for ecosystem monitoring.

Forest Resilience

Monitoring drought and disturbance impacts in temperate forests using Sentinel-2, PRISMA, and ICOS flux data.

Crop Stress Detection

Integrating hyperspectral, thermal, and UAV observations to track disease progression in agricultural systems.

SIF & Productivity

Exploring satellite-based sun-induced fluorescence as a window into ecosystem photosynthesis and GPP.
