Hybrid ML & Earth Observation for multi-stressor crop disease detection

HYDRA-EO is an ESA-funded project coordinated by Wageningen University & Research. It links hyperspectral, thermal and fluorescence data with radiative transfer models and machine learning to retrieve plant traits and detect biotic and abiotic stress in key crops across Spain, Italy and the Netherlands.

Duration: 30 months (2026–2028) Prime contractor: Wageningen University & Research (WU-DES, GRS) Partners: WENR, CNR-IBE, CIAG-IRIAF

HYDRA-EO combines UAVs, satellites, RTMs and genetics to understand plant stress.

Scientific objectives

HYDRA-EO develops a hybrid modelling framework that couples radiative transfer simulations with machine learning to retrieve functional plant traits and disentangle overlapping stress signals (drought vs. disease) across multiple EO platforms and crops.

Traits

Retrieve key functional traits

Retrieve chlorophyll content, LAI, water status, canopy temperature, nitrogen indices, and SIF-related indicators from hyperspectral, multispectral and thermal data using PROSAIL and SCOPE-based simulations combined with ML.

Stressors

Disentangle drought and disease

Separate spectral and thermal fingerprints of drought, fungal infections and viral diseases, enabling early detection and improved resilience assessments in agricultural systems.

Scaling

Bridge scales & missions

Quantify how trait signals degrade from UAV and airborne hyperspectral data to satellite missions such as Sentinel-2, PRISMA, EnMAP, FLEX, CHIME and LSTM, and design optimised bandsets and indices for operational monitoring.

Impact

Operational EO tools for users

Deliver open, ESA-oriented tools and Shiny interfaces that translate advanced modelling into practical decision support for agronomists, advisors and policy stakeholders.

Crop–stressor scenarios

HYDRA-EO focuses on five strategic crops and a palette of biotic and abiotic stressors. Field campaigns and EO acquisitions are co-designed to capture contrasted responses across varieties, management and climate zones.

Alfalfa (Italy)

Drought and water-management experiments at Italian test sites are used to analyse structural and physiological changes in alfalfa using hyperspectral, thermal and flux data.

Potato (Netherlands & Spain)

Multi-stressor trials including Potato Virus Y and water stress, combining ICOS-style flux measurements, UAV imaging and Sentinel-2 / PRISMA / EnMAP observations.

Grapevine (Italy)

Vineyards infected with Flavescence Dorée and other fungal pathogens support the development of spectral–thermal indicators for early disease detection and trait retrieval in perennial crops.

Pistachio & Olive (Spain)

CIAG-IRIAF orchards with inoculated fungal and bacterial pathogens (e.g. Septoria spp., Pseudomonas savastanoi) under varying water regimes are monitored using UAV hyperspectral, thermal, and aircraft-borne sensors to build a unique multi-sensor stress library.

Methods & data

HYDRA-EO combines empirical datasets, radiative transfer models and advanced machine learning into a single workflow, aligned with ESA’s Digital Twin Earth vision.

  • Field & UAV campaigns: Coordinated hyperspectral, thermal and fluorescence measurements over alfalfa, potato, grapevine, pistachio and olive sites.
  • Airborne & satellite data: Integration of PRISMA, EnMAP, Sentinel-2, FLEX-like SIF products and simulated CHIME and LSTM configurations.
  • Radiative transfer: Use of PROSAIL and SCOPE–based models for spectral, fluorescence and thermal simulation under controlled trait–stressor combinations.
  • Hybrid ML: Random forests, gradient boosting, CNNs and Gaussian processes trained on RTM-informed synthetic libraries and empirical observations.

From physics to operational products

Simulations in WP3–WP4 generate trait-sensitive reflectance, fluorescence and thermal signatures for different sensors. WP5 trains ML models to retrieve traits and classify stressors, while WP6 packages the full pipeline into open-source R code and Shiny dashboards tailored to ESA users.

Open tools & workspaces

HYDRA-EO builds on existing open-source ecosystems developed at Wageningen University & Research and extends them with ESA-funded modules for plant trait retrieval and stress detection.

ToolsRTM (R package)

A radiative transfer modelling toolbox maintained by WU-DES for PROSAIL-based simulations, spectral resampling, LUT generation and hybrid inversion. HYDRA-EO will add new routines for trait–stressor libraries, band reduction and thermal integration.

SCOPEinR (R interface)

An R interface for SCOPE-style energy-balance and SIF simulations, used to produce consistent reflectance, fluorescence and thermal datasets for crops under varying stress and illumination conditions.

GitLab project workspace

A central GitLab repository will host the HYDRA-EO pipelines: RTM code, ML models, spectral libraries, trait-stressor metadata and Shiny applications. ESA and project partners will have full access for collaboration, reproducibility and long-term reuse.

Shiny app for trait retrieval

An interactive web application (developed in WP6) will allow users to explore retrieval workflows, visualise scaling effects, and test different sensor configurations and bandsets for specific crops and pathogens.
