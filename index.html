HYDRA-EO: Hybrid Earth Observation for early detection of crop diseases and stress.

HYDRA-EO integrates hyperspectral, thermal and multi-sensor satellite data with radiative transfer models and hybrid machine learning to detect, quantify, and map crop stress caused by multiple biotic and abiotic factors.

Mission & objectives

HYDRA-EO aims to build a transferable framework that combines high-resolution remote sensing, radiative transfer modelling, and hybrid machine learning to detect, characterise, and monitor crop stress caused by pathogens and water or heat stress. The project focuses on real agricultural systems, with contrasting resistant and susceptible varieties in Mediterranean and temperate environments.

Objective 1

Detect multiple stressors

Quantify spectral and thermal signatures of diseases and abiotic stress in orchards and field crops, disentangling overlapping signals at canopy level.

Biotic vs abiotic Spectral fingerprints
Objective 2

Hybrid ML & RTM framework

Couple radiative transfer models (e.g. PROSAIL, SCOPE) with machine learning classifiers and regressors to retrieve functional traits and stress indicators.

RTM-informed features Ensemble models
Objective 3

Operational mapping tools

Deliver EO-based tools and processing chains that can be transferred to other crops, pathogens, and regions, supporting decision-making and early warning systems.

Science & methods

HYDRA-EO combines high-resolution imaging, radiative transfer modelling, and hybrid machine learning to link canopy reflectance, fluorescence and temperature with underlying physiological processes and disease progression.

Remote sensing & modelling

Radiative transfer & physiology

Leaf and canopy radiative transfer models are used to simulate hyperspectral reflectance, sun-induced fluorescence and thermal emission under different stress scenarios. These simulations guide the selection of spectral regions and indicators most sensitive to pathogen damage, water stress and combined stressors.

PROSAIL / SCOPE Trait retrieval Sensitivity analysis
Hybrid machine learning

From pixels to stress maps

Hybrid ML models ingest spectral bands, vegetation indices, thermal and RTM-derived features to predict stress class, severity and key functional traits. Approaches include ensemble methods, CNNs for hyperspectral cubes, and RTM-informed feature engineering.

Ensemble classifiers CNN / deep learning Explainable ML

Workplan & work packages

The workplan is organised into work packages that connect field campaigns, EO data processing, modelling, and tool development into a coherent, multi-year workflow.

WP1

Field campaigns & protocols

Design and execution of coordinated field campaigns across multiple crops, including UAV flights, leaf spectroscopy, gas exchange, fluorescence, and detailed disease scoring.

WP2

EO data processing

Pre-processing of hyperspectral, thermal and satellite data, atmospheric correction, BRDF handling and generation of analysis-ready data cubes.

WP3

RTM simulations & trait retrieval

Radiative transfer simulations to support trait inversion, stress indicators and benchmarking of spectral configurations (e.g. S2, PRISMA, EnMAP, CHIME).
