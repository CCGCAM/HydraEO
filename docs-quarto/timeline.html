Project progress · Updated 27 August 2026

HYDRA-EO roadmap: campaigns, modelling, validation, and transfer

HYDRA-EO is moving from its start-up and first campaign phase into the development of harmonised datasets, RTM-informed trait retrieval, and multi-site validation. This page connects project phases with concrete scientific and dissemination milestones.

Current activities

Late summer 2026 · Multi-country campaigns and the RTM-Suite release are completed or under way. The project is now turning these observations into a harmonised analysis-ready dataset and an operational processing chain.

Hyperspectral processing

Processing and quality control of field, UAV, airborne, and satellite hyperspectral observations, together with thermal and fluorescence products.

Integrated database

Building the project database that connects leaf and canopy measurements, plant physiology, biochemistry, disease assessments, and multi-scale EO data.

Processing & retrieval chain

Creating the reproducible HYDRA-EO pipeline for spectral harmonisation, RTM simulation, trait retrieval, and hybrid RTM-ML stress detection.

Milestones in 2026

January-June 2026

Project foundations

Scientific requirements, use cases, crop-stressor priorities, consortium coordination, campaign design, and initial integration of modelling and Earth Observation workflows.

Completed

May-July 2026

Five Lelystad potato UAV campaigns

WU-DES conducted campaigns on 11 May, 16 June, 25 June, 1 July, and 21 July, combining VNIR hyperspectral, thermal, fluorescence/SIF, RGB, disease, and plant measurements. The final campaign supported comparison with the 19 July EnMAP acquisition.

Explore the campaign story

Completed

June-August 2026

First airborne campaign cycle

Campaigns at El Chaparrillo, Valencia, and the Bologna region expanded the project across potato, olive, pistachio, and vineyard systems, including collaboration with CERBERUS, CIAG-IRIAF, IAS-CSIC, and regional plant-health partners.

Completed

9-14 August 2026

Two contributions presented at IGARSS 2026

HYDRA-EO research on forest productivity and early fungal detection was presented in Washington, D.C. The conference papers are available from the project News page.

View the conference outputs

Completed

27 August 2026

RTM-Suite public release

The R and Python ecosystem now connects leaf, canopy, soil, atmosphere, energy-balance, fluorescence, sensor convolution, and plant-trait inversion with 31 reproducible tutorials and interactive applications.

Planned repeat airborne and field acquisitions over olive and pistachio systems, combining VNIR-SWIR hyperspectral and thermal observations with physiological, biochemical, and disease reference measurements.

Work-package status

The ten work packages operate as a connected programme rather than a strictly sequential list. Their current emphasis is summarised below.

Foundations & coordination Active throughout

  • WP1 - Scientific requirement consolidation
  • WP10 - Project management, reporting, risk, and contractual coordination

Observations & experimental data Primary 2026 focus

  • WP2 - Field, UAV, airborne, satellite, and in-situ dataset collection
  • WP4 - Experimental EO products, synthetic data, and stress-specific libraries

Models & scientific exploitation 2026-2027

  • WP3 - RTM, sensitivity, sensor comparison, inversion, and validation
  • WP5 - Trait analysis, stress dynamics, and UAV-to-satellite scaling

Impact, collaboration & roadmap Growing toward 2028

  • WP6 - Agricultural relevance and stakeholder transfer
  • WP7 - Scientific roadmap and recommendations for future missions
  • WP8 - Collaboration with ESA, European projects, and research networks
  • WP9 - Publications, conferences, training, open tools, and outreach
